ABOUT ME

Public Health & Field Epidemiology

Professional Profile

I am currently studying Field Epidemiology at Haramaya University. My professional interests focus on applying epidemiological methods and health data to understand disease patterns and support evidence-based public health decisions.

My interests include disease surveillance, malaria epidemiology, climate and health, epidemiological research, time-series analysis, forecasting, spatial analysis, and health data analysis.

My goal is to contribute to practical public health research that generates reliable evidence for disease prevention, surveillance, and health decision-making.
