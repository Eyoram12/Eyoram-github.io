Professional Profile

I am a Field Epidemiology and Public Health researcher from Ethiopia with an interest in applied epidemiology, disease surveillance, malaria epidemiology, climate and health, and health data analysis.


My research interests include using epidemiological methods, statistical analysis, time-series approaches, and forecasting to support evidence-based public health decision-making.
