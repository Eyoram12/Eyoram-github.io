Public Health and Field Epidemiology

A professional research portfolio focused on epidemiological evidence, disease surveillance and health data.

Research with a population health focus

My work focuses on understanding disease patterns, identifying important risk factors and supporting evidence-based public health decision-making.

I am a Field Epidemiology student and public health professional from Ethiopia with interests in epidemiological research and disease surveillance.

My research interests include malaria epidemiology, climate and health, temporal and seasonal analysis, forecasting, spatial analysis and health data analysis.

I am particularly interested in using routine health data and epidemiological methods to understand disease patterns and generate evidence for public health planning.
