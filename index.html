About My Research

My research activities focus on understanding disease patterns, identifying factors associated with disease occurrence, evaluating disease surveillance systems, and applying epidemiological and statistical methods to public health data.

My current research interests include malaria epidemiology, climate variability and health, disease surveillance, time-series analysis, seasonal patterns, spatial analysis, health data analysis, and forecasting.

The projects presented below represent my current and recent research activities across different areas of field epidemiology and public health.

Current and Recent Research Projects

Selected research projects covering malaria epidemiology, climate and health, disease surveillance, and priority disease analysis.

PROJECT 01

Attribution of Climate Variability to Malaria Incidence and Forecasting for Further Interventions in East Hararghe Zone, Oromia Region, Ethiopia (2016–2025)

This study focuses on assessing the relationship between climate variables and malaria incidence and developing a forecasting model to support malaria prevention and control.

Study Area East Hararghe Zone, Oromia Region, Ethiopia
Study Period 2016–2025
Main Objective To assess the effect of climate variables on malaria incidence and develop a forecasting model to support malaria prevention and control efforts in East Hararghe Zone, Oromia, Ethiopia, using data from 2016 to 2025.
Data Sources DHIS2 malaria data, Ethiopian Meteorological Agency climate data, and relevant intervention data.
Methods Retrospective ecological time-series analysis, climate-malaria association analysis, lag analysis, and forecasting.
Data Collection
PROJECT 02

Determinants of Malaria in Haramaya District, Oromia Region, Eastern Ethiopia, 2026

This research focuses on identifying factors associated with malaria among the study population in Haramaya District.

Study Area Haramaya District, Oromia Region, Eastern Ethiopia
Study Year 2026
Research Focus Assessment of risk factors associated with malaria in Haramaya District, Oromia Region, Eastern Ethiopia.
Journal Journal of Interventional Epidemiology and Public Health (JIEPH)
Publication Status Manuscript under editorial and peer-review processes.
Under Review
PROJECT 03

Malaria Surveillance System Evaluation and Trends in Kombolcha District, East Hararghe Zone, Oromia Region, Ethiopia, 2026

This study evaluates the malaria surveillance system and examines malaria trends in Kombolcha District.

Study Area Kombolcha District, East Hararghe Zone, Oromia Region, Ethiopia
Study Year 2026
Data Malaria surveillance data.
Research Focus Evaluation of malaria surveillance system performance and analysis of malaria trends using available surveillance data.
Methods Surveillance system evaluation and malaria trend analysis.
Manuscript Writing
PROJECT 04

Temporal, Seasonal, and Spatial Dynamics of Priority Diseases in East Bale Zone, Oromia Region, Ethiopia, 2023–2026

This research examines temporal trends, seasonal patterns, and spatial distribution of priority diseases across East Bale Zone.

Study Area East Bale Zone, Oromia Region, Ethiopia
Study Period 2023–2026
Data Health surveillance data.
Research Focus Analysis of temporal, seasonal, and spatial patterns of priority diseases using health surveillance data from 2023 to 2026.
Methods Temporal trend analysis, seasonal analysis, and spatial analysis.
Manuscript Editing

Research Methods and Analytical Approaches

My research work applies quantitative epidemiological and statistical approaches to understand disease patterns, determinants, surveillance performance, and future trends.

  • Retrospective ecological studies
  • Time-series analysis
  • Temporal trend analysis
  • Seasonal pattern analysis
  • Spatial analysis and mapping
  • Climate and disease association analysis
  • Lag-effect analysis
  • Forecasting and predictive modelling
  • Disease surveillance system evaluation
  • Health data analysis
  • Statistical analysis using Stata and R
  • GIS analysis using ArcGIS Pro

Evidence-Based Public Health Research

My research aims to generate practical epidemiological evidence that can support disease prevention, surveillance improvement, health planning, and public health decision-making.
