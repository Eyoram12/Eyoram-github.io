About Me

Field Epidemiology and Public Health Research

I am Getahun Bedada Feye, a Field Epidemiology student and public health professional from Ethiopia. My professional interests focus on epidemiology, disease surveillance, malaria, climate and health, and the use of health data to support evidence-based public health decisions.

My research interests include understanding disease patterns, identifying determinants, evaluating public health surveillance systems, analysing temporal and seasonal variation, studying climate-related health effects, and developing forecasting models.

I am particularly interested in applying epidemiological methods, statistical analysis, routine health data, environmental information, and spatial analysis to generate practical evidence for public health programs.
