Research Overview

Evidence-focused epidemiological research

My research focuses on generating and analysing epidemiological evidence to support disease surveillance, prevention, planning, and public health decision-making.

Research direction

My current work combines field epidemiology, routine health data, climate information, surveillance data, temporal analysis, forecasting, and spatial methods to understand disease patterns and support evidence-based public health action.

Data-driven Uses available epidemiological and health information to answer practical questions.
Quantitative Emphasizes statistical analysis and reproducible analytical approaches.
Public health focused Connects research findings with surveillance, prevention, and planning.

Research approach

The research process is structured around clear epidemiological questions, reliable data sources, appropriate quantitative methods, and interpretation of findings for public health use.

STEP 01 Research Question

Define the epidemiological problem and analytical objectives.

STEP 02 Data

Identify, organize, and assess relevant health and environmental data.

STEP 03 Analysis

Apply appropriate statistical, temporal, and spatial methods.

STEP 04 Evidence

Interpret findings and communicate implications for public health.
