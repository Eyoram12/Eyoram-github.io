Research Overview

Evidence-based public health research

My research interests combine epidemiological methods, health data analysis, statistical modelling, surveillance evaluation, and spatial analysis.

Research Perspective

I am interested in understanding how diseases change over time and across locations, how environmental factors influence disease patterns, and how routinely collected health data can be transformed into useful evidence for public health action.

Quantitative Approach

My work uses retrospective ecological studies, time-series analysis, statistical modelling, forecasting, temporal trend analysis, seasonal analysis, spatial analysis, and surveillance indicators.
