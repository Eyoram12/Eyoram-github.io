Research, Epidemiology & Public Health

My research focuses on malaria epidemiology, disease surveillance, climate and health, temporal and seasonal patterns, forecasting, spatial analysis, and health data analysis.

Research Overview

Evidence-Based Epidemiological Research

My research uses epidemiological methods and health data to understand disease patterns, determinants, surveillance performance and future disease trends.

Research Focus

I am interested in understanding how diseases vary across time, seasons and geographical areas, with particular attention to malaria and climate-sensitive health conditions.

Public Health Application

The goal of my research is to generate reliable epidemiological evidence that supports disease surveillance, planning, forecasting and public health decision-making.

My Research Approach

I begin with clearly defined epidemiological questions and appropriate study designs.

I use health and environmental data to describe disease patterns, examine relationships and identify important epidemiological findings.

Particular attention is given to temporal variation, seasonal patterns, geographical distribution, climate-related effects and future disease trends.

Research findings are interpreted in relation to public health needs and potential applications in disease prevention, surveillance and planning.
