Research & Publications

My research focuses on field epidemiology, malaria epidemiology, disease surveillance, climate and health, health data analysis, temporal and seasonal patterns, forecasting, spatial analysis, and evidence-based public health decision-making.

Research Overview

Selected areas of epidemiological and public health research.

Evidence-Based Public Health Research

My research interests focus on applying epidemiological methods and health data to understand disease patterns, identify determinants, evaluate surveillance systems, and support public health interventions.

Particular interests include malaria epidemiology, climate-sensitive diseases, disease surveillance, temporal trends, seasonal patterns, spatial analysis, forecasting, and statistical modelling.

My research uses routinely collected health data, environmental information, and epidemiological methods to generate practical evidence for public health decision-making.

Research Approach

My research approach combines epidemiological thinking, statistical analysis, health surveillance data, and environmental information to generate evidence that can support public health decision-making.

I focus on clearly defining research questions, selecting appropriate study designs, managing and analysing health data, interpreting statistical findings, and translating evidence into practical public health recommendations.

Particular attention is given to temporal variation, seasonal patterns, spatial distribution, climate-related health effects, surveillance performance, and forecasting of disease trends.
